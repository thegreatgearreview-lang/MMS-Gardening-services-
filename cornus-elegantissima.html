Cornus 'Elegantissima'

Cornus alba 'Elegantissima'

Cornus 'Elegantissima' is a hardy deciduous shrub with attractive green leaves edged in creamy white. It produces small creamy-white flowers in late spring and develops colourful stems that add winter interest. A reliable choice for borders, mixed planting and larger garden areas.

☀ Full sun / part shade · 🌱 Moist, well-drained soil · 🌼 Late spring flowering · 🍂 Deciduous · 💪 Hardy
