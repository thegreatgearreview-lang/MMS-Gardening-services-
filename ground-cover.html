Ground Cover Plants

Low-growing plants that spread across borders and beds, helping cover bare soil while adding colour, texture and interest. Succulents such as Sedum and Sempervivum are especially useful for sunny, well-drained rockeries and gravel areas.

Succulents & Rockery Ground Cover

Hardy, low-growing succulents and alpine-style plants can fill gaps between rocks and soften the edges of paths, borders and raised beds.
