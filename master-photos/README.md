# MMS MASTER PHOTOS

This is the permanent master-photo folder for MMS Gardening Services.

Photos added here are to be treated as **MMS master photos** and reused on the website until Mark specifically says to replace one.

## Categories
- `hedges/domestic/` — domestic hedge master photos
- `hedges/commercial/` — commercial hedge master photos
- `planting-borders/` — planting and border master photos
- `aquatics/` — aquatics and water-feature master photos
- `commercial/` — commercial grounds master photos
- `nursery/` — nursery and plant master photos
- `other/` — other approved MMS master photos

**Important:** Do not replace a master photo with a generic image unless Mark specifically asks for a temporary replacement.
